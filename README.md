# 🏓 Pong Game

A classic Pong game built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Play against an AI opponent!

## 🎮 Play Online

**[Click here to play the game](https://shubham-k-jha.github.io/pong/)**

## 🎯 How to Play

### Controls
- **Mouse**: Move your paddle by moving your mouse up and down
- **Keyboard**: Use **↑ Arrow Up** or **W** to move up, **↓ Arrow Down** or **S** to move down

### Objective
- Keep the ball from passing your paddle (left side)
- Score points when the ball passes the computer's paddle (right side)
- First to get the highest score wins!

### Game Features
- ✅ Player-controlled left paddle (mouse + keyboard)
- ✅ AI-controlled right paddle (computer opponent)
- ✅ Bouncing ball with physics
- ✅ Collision detection for paddles and walls
- ✅ Real-time scoreboard
- ✅ Increasing difficulty as ball speeds up

## 📋 Tips
- Use your mouse for better control
- Position your paddle at center for maximum reaction time
- Hit the ball at different heights of your paddle to change direction
- Watch the ball's trajectory and anticipate where it's going

## 🛠️ Technologies Used
- HTML5
- CSS3
- Vanilla JavaScript (no dependencies)
